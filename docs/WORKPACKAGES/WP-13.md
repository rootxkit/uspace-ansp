## What to build

### Deployment

- `deploy/compose.prod.yaml`: the `PLAN §11` services with resource
  limits, the one-shot `migrate` service both long-running services
  depend on (M36), one `timescale/timescaledb-ha:pg16` container
  holding both databases (M37), the real adapter service template,
  read-only root filesystems, the NATS credentials and secrets as
  files; `deploy/caddy/ansp.caddy` as a **snippet** the private
  deployment repo `uspace-deploy` composes into the droplet's shared
  Caddyfile (D1 of the reconciliation; this repo never runs Caddy
  alone): the route groups, `client_auth { mode verify_if_given,
  trusted_ca_cert_file ... }` with the CA from `uspace-deploy`,
  `X-Client-Cert-Subject` forwarded on `/v1/manned-traffic/*` and
  `/v1/coordination/*` and stripped on every other route, `/metrics`
  never routed; the Go side enforces presence per `ANSP_MTLS_MODE`
  (`required` in production, `off` on the staging droplet and in the
  lab, M25). The console's `/basemap/*` is served by `uspace-deploy`'s
  Caddy from the shared read-only basemap volume (M38); this snippet
  only documents the dependency.
- CI `image` job completed: build on `main` and `v*` tags, push
  `ghcr.io/rootxkit/uspace-ansp:<sha>` and `:<tag>` and the web image,
  `cosign sign` (keyless), SBOM (`syft`) attached; a `deploy/verify.sh`
  that checks the signature before `compose pull`.
- `deploy/backup.sh` (nightly `pg_dump` + Timescale chunk backup to the
  second account), `deploy/rollback.md` (by image tag).
- `make conformance-target`: brings up the stack with lab-issued test
  keys and the replay adapter, prints the base URL and the client ids
  for `uspace-lab/conformance/`; `testdata/conformance/` holds the
  configuration the suite needs (constraint-management checks against
  the ANSP and the lab DSS; the national OpenAPI contract tests).

### Proof runbooks

`docs/runbooks/n-m1.md`:

1. Baseline (E-06): the stack idle for 10 min; counters at zero; the
   feed status says `adapters: replay running`, the picture empty with
   the SC-22 text.
2. A SITL aircraft (lab) airborne inside the fixture U-space airspace
   with an activated intent at the USSP.
3. A supervisor activates a restriction over it from the console.
   Record: the restriction's commit time (events), the CISP's `201`
   time (deliveries), the CISP's change notification time (its log),
   the DSS `PUT` time and the subscriber `POST` times, the USSP's
   `restriction_activated` alert time (its stream), the authority's
   display time. Pass: CISP ≤ 1 s, DSS + notify ≤ 5 s, USSP alert
   within one tick.
4. The replay adapter streams `helicopter-near-uspace.ndjson` and
   `two-aircraft-converging.ndjson`; the USSP's traffic WS and the
   authority's picture show them as `surveillance` with ages; a USSP CPA
   alert against the SITL aircraft is observed (the USSP's scenario; we
   record that our feed was its input).
5. End the restriction; record the same chain for deactivation.
6. Every delivery row `sent`; `make verify-audit` (hash chain) ok.

`docs/runbooks/n-m2.md`: the Annex V inbox receiving the USSP's intent
notice and a non-conformance notice (the lab flies the SITL aircraft out
of its volume), `ack_id` returned, supervisor acknowledgement observed by
the USSP; the CISP stopped for 2 min → alarm at 10 s, direct deliveries
to the USSP and the authority logged and verified, reconciliation after
restart; SC-15 (adapter paused 30 s; nothing live from old positions);
SC-08 (adapter switched off and on; `source_disabled` ageing; other
adapter untouched); an occurrence report delivered to the authority;
memory and CPU of the ANSP stack over the run (gap 20).

`docs/runbooks/release.md`: how to tag, what CI does on a tag, how to
verify an image, how to roll back.
